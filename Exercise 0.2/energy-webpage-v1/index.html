Australian Appliance Energy Awareness

Smarter energy choices start at home.

This website highlights how common household appliances affect electricity use in the Australian market, with a focus on televisions, efficiency, and everyday energy-saving habits.

Why it matters

Appliances can quietly add to household power bills, especially when they are used for long periods or left on standby.

What to look for

Energy rating labels, screen size, usage habits, and standby power all play a role in total consumption.

Televisions

Energy use and TV buying habits in Australia

Televisions vary widely in energy use depending on screen size, display type, brightness settings, and how many hours they are used each day. Larger screens often use more electricity, while modern efficient models can reduce long-term operating costs.

Screen size

Big screens usually consume more power than compact models.

Standby mode

Even when off, some TVs still draw a small amount of electricity.

Energy rating

Higher-rated models are generally more efficient over time.
